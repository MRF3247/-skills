## Description: <br>
AI video generation and production powered by CellCog for marketing videos, product demos, explainers, educational content, lipsync spokesperson videos, UGC, news reports, training materials, cinematic short films, social media reels, and YouTube content up to 4 minutes from a single prompt. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, creators, marketers, educators, and developers use this skill to ask an agent to generate scripted, voiced, scored, and edited videos through CellCog from concise creative prompts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Video prompts, scripts, and related creative inputs are sent to CellCog. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid including private, regulated, or sensitive information unless the CellCog account terms and data-handling expectations allow it. <br>
Risk: Video generation can consume paid CellCog credits without guaranteeing a usable result. <br>
Mitigation: Start with small, well-scoped prompts and confirm cost expectations before requesting longer or more complex videos. <br>
Risk: Long-form AI video quality is unpredictable and may not meet production expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated videos before publication and iterate on duration, aspect ratio, style, audio, and key scene requirements. <br>
